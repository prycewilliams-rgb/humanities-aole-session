What is Assessment for Learning in Curriculum for Wales? Beth yw Asesu ar gyfer Dysgu yng Nghwricwlwm i Gymru?

⚠️ Important Context · Cyd-destun Pwysig

English: The term "Assessment for Learning" (AfL) is not used explicitly in the Curriculum for Wales (2022). Instead, the same principles are embedded in the concept of supporting learner progression — which is the statutory framework for assessment in Wales. As a PGCE student in the Humanities AoLE, you must understand both the research roots of AfL and how these principles operate within the CfW statutory guidance.

Cymraeg: Nid yw'r term "Asesu ar gyfer Dysgu" (AfL) yn cael ei ddefnyddio'n benodol yng Nghwricwlwm i Gymru (2022). Yn hytrach, mae'r egwyddorion yn ymwneud â chefnogi cynnydd dysgwyr — y fframwaith statudol ar gyfer asesu yng Nghymru.

The Research Foundation · Sylfaen yr Ymchwil

Black & Wiliam (1998) — Inside the Black Box

Landmark research showing that improving formative assessment raises standards of achievement across all abilities, but particularly for lower attaining pupils. They define formative assessment as becoming effective only when "the evidence is actually used to adapt the teaching and learning to meet the needs of individual pupils." Ymchwil arloesol yn dangos bod gwella asesu ffurfiannol yn codi safonau cyrhaeddiad ar draws pob gallu, yn enwedig ar gyfer disgyblion llai abl.

Assessment Reform Group (2002) — 10 Principles of AfL

"Assessment for Learning is the process of seeking and interpreting evidence for use by learners and their teachers to decide where the learners are in their learning, where they need to go next, and how best to get them there." "Asesu ar gyfer Dysgu yw'r broses o geisio a dehongli tystiolaeth i'w defnyddio gan ddysgwyr a'u hathrawon i benderfynu ble mae'r dysgwyr yn eu dysgu, i ble y mae angen iddynt fynd nesaf, a sut orau i'w cyrraedd yno."

Curriculum for Wales — Supporting Learner Progression: Assessment Guidance (Statutory, 2022)

Assessment in CfW has three main roles: (1) supporting individual learners on an ongoing, day-to-day basis; (2) identifying, capturing and reflecting on individual learner progress over time; (3) understanding group progress in order to reflect on practice. Assessment should be embedded into day-to-day practice, making it "indistinguishable from learning." Mae gan asesu yng Nghwricwlwm i Gymru dair rôl: (1) cefnogi dysgwyr unigol ar sail barhaus, ddyddiol; (2) nodi, dal a myfyrio ar gynnydd dysgwyr unigol dros amser; (3) deall cynnydd grŵp er mwyn myfyrio ar arfer.

Questioning & Oracy in Humanities Cwestiynu a Llafar yn y Dyniaethau

🇬🇧 English

Questioning is one of the most powerful AfL tools available to a Humanities teacher. CfW assessment guidance emphasises that practitioners should evaluate learning through observation, questioning and discussion and use this evidence to plan next steps. Effective questioning in Humanities must promote the enquiry and investigation at the heart of the first Statement of What Matters.

Black and Wiliam (1998) found teachers ask a question every 72 seconds, yet 38% of those questions are answered by the teacher themselves. The goal is to draw maximum evidence from pupils to evaluate, plan and extend their learning.

🏴󠁧󠁢󠁷󠁬󠁳󠁿 Cymraeg

Mae cwestiynu yn un o'r offer AdD mwyaf pwerus sydd ar gael i athro'r Dyniaethau. Mae canllawiau asesu CfW yn pwysleisio y dylai ymarferwyr werthuso dysgu trwy arsylwi, cwestiynu a thrafodaeth a defnyddio'r dystiolaeth hon i gynllunio camau nesaf.

Canfu Black a Wiliam (1998) fod athrawon yn gofyn cwestiwn bob 72 eiliad, ond mae 38% o'r cwestiynau hynny yn cael eu hateb gan yr athro ei hun.

Key Questioning Strategies for Humanities · Strategaethau Cwestiynu Allweddol ar gyfer y Dyniaethau

⏸️

Pose · Pause · Pounce · Bounce

Pose a challenging question; pause (at least 20 seconds — silence is thinking time); pounce on a named pupil; bounce the response to another. No hands up. All must think.

Gosod cwestiwn heriol; seibio; "pounce" ar ddisgybl; "bounce" yr ymateb i un arall. Dim dwylo i fyny.
🎨

Concept Cartoons · Cartwnau Cysyniad

Give learners competing viewpoints to discuss — ideal for Geography fieldwork debates, History interpretations or RVE ethical dilemmas. Removes fear of failure.

Rhowch safbwyntiau cystadleuol i ddysgwyr eu trafod — delfrydol ar gyfer dadleuon Daearyddiaeth, dehongliadau Hanes neu ddilemâu moesegol GAE.
🔲

KWHL Grid

Know · Want to know · How I will find out · Learnt. Activates prior knowledge and motivates enquiry — connects directly to the Humanities enquiry Statement of What Matters.

Gwybod · Eisiau gwybod · Sut y canfyddaf · Dysgais. Yn actifadu gwybodaeth flaenorol ac yn ysgogi ymholiad.
💭

Think · Pair · Share · Rhannu

Think individually; discuss with a partner; share with the class. Gives all learners — including EAL and lower confidence pupils — time to formulate sophisticated responses.

Meddwl yn unigol; trafod gyda phartner; rhannu â'r dosbarth. Yn rhoi amser i bob dysgwr lunio ymatebion.
🧱

Question Wall · Wal Gwestiynau

Pupils write their most burning question about a topic on a sticky note. Group by theme. Use as a lesson starter, mini-plenary, or basis for extended Humanities enquiry.

Disgyblion yn ysgrifennu eu cwestiwn mwyaf llosgi ar nodyn gludiog. Grwpio yn ôl thema. Defnyddio fel dechreuwr gwers neu ymholiad estynedig.
🎲

Random Name Generator · Generadur Enwau

Removes opt-out culture. Every pupil knows they could be chosen — increasing individual accountability. Pair with wait time for maximum effect.

Yn dileu'r diwylliant optio allan. Mae pob disgybl yn gwybod y gallent gael eu dewis — yn cynyddu atebolrwydd unigol. Pâriwch ag amser aros.

🏴󠁧󠁢󠁷󠁬󠁳󠁿 Oracy & the Welsh Language in Humanities · Llafar a'r Iaith Gymraeg yn y Dyniaethau

English: CfW places strong emphasis on developing oracy across all AoLEs. In Humanities, discussion and collaborative talk are essential for developing the enquiry, interpretation and citizenship skills described in the Statements of What Matters. Ensure questioning strategies provide opportunities for learners to develop both English and Welsh academic language — including discipline-specific vocabulary for History (e.g. chronology, causation), Geography (sustainability, cynefin) and RVE (worldview, ethics).

Cymraeg: Mae CfW yn rhoi pwyslais cryf ar ddatblygu llafar ar draws pob MDPH. Yn y Dyniaethau, mae trafodaeth a siarad cydweithredol yn hanfodol ar gyfer datblygu sgiliau ymholiad, dehongli a dinasyddiaeth.

Feedback & Supporting Learner Progression Adborth a Chefnogi Cynnydd Dysgwyr

🇬🇧 English — CfW Assessment Guidance

CfW requires practitioners to provide relevant and focused feedback that actively engages learners, encourages them to take responsibility for their learning, and moves their learning forward. Feedback must be used to identify the learner's starting point, their next steps, and any interventions or support required — building a holistic picture over time.

Crucially, assessment should not be "end loaded" — it must be frequent and embedded throughout learning. Practitioners must give learners time to read and act upon feedback as part of day-to-day practice.

🏴󠁧󠁢󠁷󠁬󠁳󠁥 Cymraeg — Canllawiau Asesu CfW

Mae CfW yn ei gwneud yn ofynnol i ymarferwyr ddarparu adborth perthnasol a phwrpasol sy'n ymgysylltu â dysgwyr yn weithredol, yn eu hannog i gymryd cyfrifoldeb am eu dysgu, ac yn symud eu dysgu ymlaen.

Yn hollbwysig, ni ddylai asesu fod wedi'i "lwytho ar y diwedd" — rhaid iddo fod yn aml ac wedi'i ymgorffori trwy gydol dysgu. Rhaid i ymarferwyr roi amser i ddysgwyr ddarllen ac ymateb i adborth fel rhan o arfer beunyddiol.

Feedback Strategies for Humanities · Strategaethau Adborth ar gyfer y Dyniaethau

⭐

Two Stars & a Wish · Dwy Seren a Dymuniad

Identify two specific strengths (stars) and one targeted improvement (wish). Each star must name a specific skill being mastered — not vague praise. The pupil must act on the wish immediately.

Nodi dwy gryfder penodol a gwelliant wedi'i dargedu. Rhaid i'r disgybl weithredu ar y "dymuniad" ar unwaith.
🔒

Closing the Gap · Cau'r Bwlch

Focused comments that help pupils close the gap between current and potential performance. Use: Reminder prompts, Scaffolded prompts, Example prompts. Acting on feedback is closing the gap.

Sylwadau wedi'u canolbwyntio sy'n helpu disgyblion i gau'r bwlch rhwng perfformiad cyfredol a photensial. Gweithredu ar adborth yw cau'r bwlch.
📊

RAG Rating · Sgorio CAG

Red = struggled / Amber = some areas need revisiting / Green = confident to progress. Pupils self-assess against success criteria. Gives teacher an immediate whole-class picture.

Coch = anawsterau / Amber = rhai meysydd i'w hadolygu / Gwyrdd = hyderus i symud ymlaen.
🔍

Mini Plenaries · Plenaryau Bach

Regular whole-class "check-points" during the lesson — not just at the end. Review progress against learning intentions and success criteria. Allows pupils to correct misconceptions within the lesson.

Pwyntiau gwirio rheolaidd yn ystod y wers — nid ar y diwedd yn unig. Yn caniatáu i ddisgyblion gywiro camdybiaethau yn ystod y wers.
🚪

Exit Tickets · Tocynnau Ymadael

Before leaving, pupils submit a response to a key question, problem, or reflection prompt. Gives the teacher direct evidence of what was understood — and what wasn't — to plan the next lesson.

Cyn gadael, mae disgyblion yn cyflwyno ymateb i gwestiwn allweddol. Yn rhoi tystiolaeth uniongyrchol i'r athro ar gyfer cynllunio'r wers nesaf.
💬

WWW · EBI

What Went Well — Even Better If. Structured reflection that makes improvement specific and actionable. Pupils identify both success and a concrete next step.

Beth Aeth yn Dda — Gallai fod yn Well pe bai. Adlewyrchiad strwythuredig sy'n gwneud gwelliant yn benodol ac yn weithredadwy.

⚠️ Feedback in Humanities: Key CfW Requirements · Adborth yn y Dyniaethau: Gofynion Allweddol CfW

English: CfW guidance is explicit: feedback must not focus on marks or grades as these have a negative effect on learning. In Humanities, feedback should refer directly to the discipline-specific skills in the Statements of What Matters — for example, a History pupil's ability to evaluate the validity of sources, or a Geography pupil's ability to analyse the interconnections between humans and the natural world. Practitioners must build in dedicated response time for pupils to act on written or verbal feedback.

Cymraeg: Mae canllawiau CfW yn glir: ni ddylai adborth ganolbwyntio ar farciau neu raddau gan fod y rhain yn cael effaith negyddol ar ddysgu. Yn y Dyniaethau, dylai adborth gyfeirio'n uniongyrchol at y sgiliau disgybliaeth-benodol yn y Datganiadau o'r Hyn sy'n Bwysig.

Peer & Self-Assessment in Humanities Asesu Cymheiriaid a Hunanasesu yn y Dyniaethau

🇬🇧 English — CfW Statutory Requirement

CfW assessment guidance requires practitioners to "provide opportunities for learners to engage in assessing their own work and that of their peers, and supporting them to develop the relevant skills to do this effectively." This is not optional — it is a statutory expectation. As learners progress along the 3–16 continuum, they should engage more directly in the assessment process, developing increasing independence.

🏴󠁧󠁢󠁷󠁬󠁳󠁿 Cymraeg — Gofyniad Statudol CfW

Mae canllawiau asesu CfW yn mynnu bod ymarferwyr yn "darparu cyfleoedd i ddysgwyr ymgysylltu ag asesu eu gwaith eu hunain a gwaith eu cymheiriaid, a'u cefnogi i ddatblygu'r sgiliau perthnasol i wneud hyn yn effeithiol." Nid yw hwn yn ddewisol — mae'n ddisgwyliad statudol.

📋 The Value of Peer & Self-Assessment · Gwerth Asesu Cymheiriaid a Hunanasesu

For learners · Ar gyfer dysgwyr:

  • Become responsible for their own learning · Dod yn gyfrifol am eu dysgu eu hunain
  • Can recognise next steps in their own learning with practice · Gallu adnabod camau nesaf yn eu dysgu eu hunain
  • Develop an "I can..." culture, raising self-esteem · Datblygu diwylliant "Gallaf...", gan godi hunan-barch
  • Become more independent and motivated · Dod yn fwy annibynnol ac wedi'u cymell

For teachers · Ar gyfer athrawon:

  • Responsibility shifts from teacher to pupil · Symuda cyfrifoldeb o'r athro i'r disgybl
  • Lessons become more effective as pupils become more independent · Mae gwersi'n dod yn fwy effeithiol
  • More efficient in identifying learners' next steps · Mwy effeithlon wrth nodi camau nesaf dysgwyr

Peer & Self-Assessment Strategies · Strategaethau Asesu Cymheiriaid a Hunanasesu

🚦

Traffic Lights · Goleuadau Traffig

Pupils indicate understanding using Red / Amber / Green. Simple but powerful — gives the teacher an immediate visual of the whole class. Accessible for all learners, including those with SEN.

Disgyblion yn nodi dealltwriaeth trwy Goch / Ambr / Gwyrdd. Syml ond pwerus — yn rhoi golwg weledol o'r dosbarth cyfan i'r athro.
📋

Placemats · Matiau Lle

Pupils assess work against clear criteria by highlighting "achieved" sections. Unapplied criteria form future targets. Particularly effective in Humanities for structured essay or enquiry writing.

Disgyblion yn asesu gwaith yn erbyn meini prawf clir trwy amlygu adrannau "wedi'u cyflawni". Yn arbennig o effeithiol yn y Dyniaethau ar gyfer ysgrifennu traethawd neu ymholiad strwythuredig.
💌

Exit Tickets (Peer) · Tocynnau Cymheiriad

Learners swap exit tickets with a partner and give one specific improvement suggestion before leaving. Promotes oracy and collaborative assessment — both key to the Humanities AoLE.

Mae dysgwyr yn cyfnewid tocynnau gyda phartner ac yn rhoi un awgrym gwella penodol. Yn hybu llafar ac asesu cydweithredol.
📱

Mini-Tweet Reflection · Adlewyrchiad Mini-Drydariad

Pupils summarise their learning in a limited number of words (e.g. 30 words). Forces crystallisation of key concepts — ideal for distilling historical arguments, geographical analysis or ethical standpoints.

Mae disgyblion yn crynhoi eu dysgu mewn nifer cyfyngedig o eiriau. Yn gorfodi crynhoad o gysyniadau allweddol — delfrydol yn y Dyniaethau.
👥

Peer Marking with Success Criteria · Marcio Cymheiriaid

Pupils mark a partner's work against co-constructed success criteria, then explain their evaluation and suggest one specific improvement. Must use agreed disciplinary vocabulary.

Disgyblion yn marcio gwaith partner yn erbyn meini prawf llwyddiant cyd-adeiladwyd, yna'n egluro eu gwerthusiad ac yn awgrymu un gwelliant penodol.
🔁

Talk Partners · Partneriaid Siarad

Share three new things learned; what was easy; what was challenging; what to learn next. Prompt cards focus discussion. Connects to CfW's emphasis on developing learners' ability to articulate their own progress.

Rhannu tair peth newydd a ddysgwyd; beth oedd yn hawdd; beth oedd yn heriol; beth i'w ddysgu nesaf. Yn cysylltu â phwyslais CfW ar ddatblygu gallu dysgwyr i fynegi eu cynnydd eu hunain.

⚠️ Scaffolding Peer & Self-Assessment · Sgaffaldio Asesu Cymheiriaid a Hunanasesu

English: Learners must be taught how to peer and self-assess effectively — it is a skill that develops over time. Initially pupils may give vague responses like "it's good because it's good." Start with co-constructed success criteria, concrete examples of high-quality work, and structured sentence stems. Over time, learners develop increasingly sophisticated and useful reflections — supporting the CfW principle that learners should develop increasing independence as they progress along the 3–16 continuum.

Cymraeg: Rhaid dysgu sut i asesu cymheiriaid a hunanasesu yn effeithiol i ddysgwyr — mae'n sgil sy'n datblygu dros amser. Yn y pen draw, mae dysgwyr yn datblygu adlewyrchiadau fwyfwy soffistigedig — gan gefnogi egwyddor CfW o annibyniaeth gynyddol.
