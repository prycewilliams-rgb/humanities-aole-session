What is Differentiation? Beth yw Gwahaniaethu?

🇬🇧 English

Differentiation is nothing new — it has been an integral part of good teaching for many years, mentioned as far back as 1931 in the Hadow Report. The Government White Paper (DES, 1985) confirmed its importance, defining it as matching what is taught and how it is taught to pupils' abilities and aptitudes.

Petty (2004) emphasises that differentiation should be seen as integral to learning — not a troubleshooting add-on when things go wrong. It is an inclusive concept that applies to everyone in the class.

🏴󠁧󠁢󠁷󠁬󠁳󠁿 Cymraeg

Nid yw gwahaniaethu yn newydd — mae wedi bod yn rhan annatod o addysgu da ers blynyddoedd, a soniwyd amdano mor bell â 1931. Diffiniodd y Papur Gwyn (DES, 1985) ef fel cyfateb beth sy'n cael ei addysgu a sut mae'n cael ei addysgu i alluoedd disgyblion.

Mae Petty (2004) yn pwysleisio y dylid ystyried gwahaniaethu fel rhan annatod o ddysgu — nid ychwanegiad datrys problemau. Mae'n gysyniad cynhwysol sy'n berthnasol i bawb.

Key Definitions · Diffiniadau Allweddol

Kerry (2002)

Differentiation is "the process whereby the levels of tasks set to students in class or for homework are matched to the known levels of performance and potential of the individual students involved." Y broses lle mae lefelau'r tasgau a osodir i fyfyrwyr yn cyfateb i lefelau perfformiad a photensial hysbys yr unigolion dan sylw.

Dixie (2011)

Differentiation is "teaching people differently according to their needs, their capabilities or even their preferred learning styles." The best approach is delivering the curriculum in different ways — varying both how skills and knowledge are taught and the range of tasks used to support learning. Addysgu pobl yn wahanol yn ôl eu hanghenion, eu galluoedd neu eu harddulliau dysgu ffafriedig.

Training & Development Agency for Schools

Differentiation is "the process by which differences between pupils are accommodated so that all students have the best possible chance of learning." Y broses lle caiff gwahaniaethau rhwng disgyblion eu rheoli fel bod pob myfyriwr yn cael y cyfle gorau posibl i ddysgu.

Donaldson (2015) — Independent Review of Curriculum and Assessment in Wales

Emphasises the importance of setting tasks and selecting resources that build on previous knowledge and experience and engage pupils' interests. "Children and young people arrive at school with different aspirations, interests and experiences and so will approach learning in their own particular way." Pwysleisia bwysigrwydd gosod tasgau sy'n adeiladu ar brofiad blaenorol ac yn ennyn diddordeb. Mae plant yn cyrraedd yr ysgol â gwahanol uchelgeisiau, diddordebau a phrofiadau.

💡 The Core Principle · Yr Egwyddor Ganolog

English: Consider differentiation in light of three things: pupils' readiness to learn, their learning needs, and their interests. Research shows that teaching to the average does not allow all learners to reach their full potential. Differentiation is the teacher's responsibility, and it links directly to effective Assessment for Learning.

Cymraeg: Ystyriwch wahaniaethu yng ngoleuni tair peth: parodrwydd i ddysgu, anghenion dysgu, a diddordebau disgyblion. Mae addysgu i'r cyfartaledd yn atal rhai dysgwyr rhag cyrraedd eu llawn botensial.

What Should Be Differentiated? Beth Ddylid ei Wahaniaethu?

Tomlinson's Three Components (1999) · Tri Chydran Tomlinson (1999)

🇬🇧 English

Tomlinson (1999) argues that to differentiate effectively, teachers must think about three distinct components of their teaching. These give structure to your planning and move you beyond vague intentions towards purposeful, specific differentiation.

🏴󠁧󠁢󠁷󠁬󠁳󠁿 Cymraeg

Mae Tomlinson (1999) yn dadlau y dylai athrawon feddwl am dri chydran penodol o'u haddysgu. Mae'r rhain yn rhoi strwythur i'ch cynllunio ac yn eich helpu i symud tuag at wahaniaethu bwriadol, penodol.

Component · Cydran English: What this means in practice Cymraeg: Yr hyn y mae'n ei olygu mewn ymarfer
Content
Cynnwys		 The elements and materials used to teach concepts, principles and skills. This is what pupils will learn — you may vary the texts, resources or vocabulary used with different pupils to make the content accessible. Y deunyddiau a'r elfennau a ddefnyddir i addysgu cysyniadau a sgiliau. Gallwch amrywio'r testunau, adnoddau neu eirfa a ddefnyddir gyda gwahanol ddisgyblion.
Process
Proses		 How the content is taught — for example, whole-class discussion, followed by small-group work, paired activity, or individual tasks. The process can vary to suit different learning needs even when the content is the same. Sut y caiff y cynnwys ei addysgu — er enghraifft, trafodaeth dosbarth cyfan, yna gwaith grŵp bach, gweithgaredd parau, neu dasgau unigol.
Product
Cynnyrch		 What pupils will produce to demonstrate their learning — will you allow choices in how they present their work? Written, verbal, visual? Giving pupils agency over the product can motivate and extend learning. Beth fydd disgyblion yn ei gynhyrchu i ddangos eu dysgu. A fyddwch chi'n caniatáu dewisiadau yn sut maent yn cyflwyno eu gwaith?

⚠️ Tomlinson's Caution (2013) — The VAK Neuromyth · Rhybudd Tomlinson — Niwrofyth VAK

English: Tomlinson (2013) also warns against pigeon-holing pupils by labelling them as purely visual, auditory or kinaesthetic (VAK) learners. Research by Coffield et al. (2004) shows that children do not process information more effectively when taught in their so-called "preferred learning style." This is a neuromyth — a misconception generated by a misreading of genuine brain research (OECD, 2002). The same person will learn differently in different contexts. Instead, use differentiation to give pupils options and choices, helping them to know themselves as learners.

Cymraeg: Mae Tomlinson (2013) yn rhybuddio rhag labelu disgyblion fel dysgwyr gweledol, clywedol neu ginesthetig (VAK). Mae ymchwil Coffield et al. (2004) yn dangos nad yw plant yn prosesu gwybodaeth yn fwy effeithiol yn eu "harddull dysgu ffafriedig" honedig — mae hwn yn niwrofyth. Yn hytrach, defnyddiwch wahaniaethu i roi opsiynau a dewisiadau i ddisgyblion.

The Three Broad Categories of Differentiation · Tair Categori Eang

English: All three categories should ideally be used together — do not rely on any single one.
Cymraeg: Dylid defnyddio'r tair categori gyda'i gilydd yn ddelfrydol.

By Task — Setting different tasks for different ability levels, all relating to the same topic.
Trwy Dasg — Gosod tasgau gwahanol i wahanol alluoedd ar yr un pwnc.

By Support — Providing different levels of help and scaffolding to certain pupils within the group.
Trwy Gymorth — Darparu gwahanol raddfeydd o help i rai disgyblion.

By Outcome — Same task for all; pupils respond at their own level. Use with care — not suitable for structured learning.
Trwy Ganlyniad — Yr un dasg i bawb. Defnyddiwch yn ofalus — nid addas ar gyfer dysgu strwythuredig.

🔑 Three Planning Questions · Tair Cwestiwn Cynllunio

Before each lesson, ask:

  • 1. By the end of this lesson, what should pupils know? · Beth ddylai disgyblion ei wybod?
  • 2. What should pupils be able to understand? · Beth ddylai disgyblion allu ei ddeall?
  • 3. What should pupils be able to do? · Beth ddylai disgyblion allu ei wneud?

Methods, Strategies & Questioning Dulliau, Strategaethau a Chwestiynu

Eight Key Differentiation Strategies · Wyth Strategaeth Allweddol

Strategy · StrategaethWhat it means / Yr hyn y mae'n ei olygu
Resource · AdnoddauProvide different materials for different pupils — e.g. a word bank or sentence starters for lower attaining pupils, advanced texts or research materials for more able pupils. / Darparwch ddeunyddiau gwahanol — e.e. banc geiriau i ddisgyblion llai medrus, testunau cymhletach i'r rhai mwy abl.
Intervention · YmyrraethVary the level of support, supervision, and questioning for different pupils. Modify the task when non-achievement is anticipated. Note: additional adults (LSAs) need not only support lower ability groups — they can also challenge higher ability groups. / Amrywiwch y lefel o gymorth a goruchwyliaeth. Gall oedolion ychwanegol hefyd herio grwpiau gallu uwch.
Progressive Questioning · Cwestiynu CynyddolDirect lower-order questions (knowledge, comprehension) at lower attaining pupils first; move to higher-order questions (analysis, evaluation) for higher attaining pupils. Plan key differentiated questions within your lesson plan. / Cyfeiriwch gwestiynau is-raddol at ddisgyblion llai medrus yn gyntaf; symudwch i gwestiynau uwch-raddol ar gyfer rhai mwy abl. Cynlluniwch gwestiynau allweddol yn eich cynllun gwers.
Grouping · GrwpioGroup pupils by prior attainment, interest, or task requirements. Consider whether mixed-ability or same-ability grouping better serves the specific learning objective. Donaldson (2015) warns against superficial groupings. / Grwpiwch yn ôl cyrhaeddiad, diddordeb, neu ofynion y dasg. Ystyriwch pa fath o grŵp sy'n gweddu orau i'r amcan dysgu penodol.
Task · TasgSet different tasks for different pupils on the same topic — ensuring equality of opportunity and entitlement for all. Learners may also be asked to present work in their preferred format. / Gosodwch dasgau gwahanol i ddisgyblion gwahanol ar yr un pwnc — gan sicrhau cyfle cyfartal i bawb.
A / B / C Planning · Cynllunio A/B/CA = All must (foundation tasks every pupil completes) · Rhaid i bawb
B = Most should (core tasks the majority complete) · Dylai'r rhan fwyaf
C = Some could (extension tasks for the most able — enriching the topic or increasing complexity) · Gallai rhai
Optional Activities · Gweithgareddau DewisolMeaningful extension tasks for pupils wishing to deepen learning in class or in their own time — building independence, thinking skills, and responsibility for their own learning. / Tasgau estyniad ystyrlon ar gyfer disgyblion sy'n dymuno dyfnhau eu dysgu — gan feithrin annibyniaeth.
Product / Outcome · Cynnyrch / CanlyniadAll pupils receive the same task and resources and respond at their own level, producing outcomes that reflect their ability. Suitable for open-ended, creative activities — but not helpful for structured learning requiring a specific approach. Do not rely on this as your primary strategy. / Yr un dasg a'r un adnoddau i bawb — addas ar gyfer gweithgareddau agored ond nid ar gyfer dysgu strwythuredig. Peidiwch â gordibynnu ar hyn.

Bloom's Taxonomy & Progressive Questioning · Tacsonomeg Bloom a Chwestiynu Cynyddol

🇬🇧 English

Bloom et al. (1956) identified a hierarchy of thinking skills from lower-order (knowledge, comprehension, application) to higher-order (analysis, synthesis, evaluation). Research shows teachers ask questions at the knowledge level 80–90% of the time — but higher-order questions require more thinking and lead to deeper learning.

Walsh and Sattes (2005) found that teachers typically wait less than one second after asking a question. Extended wait time — giving pupils more thinking time — leads to more pupils formulating and offering answers.

🏴󠁧󠁢󠁷󠁬󠁳󠁿 Cymraeg

Nododd Bloom et al. (1956) hierarchaeth o sgiliau meddwl o is-raddol i uwch-raddol. Mae ymchwil yn dangos bod athrawon yn gofyn cwestiynau ar lefel gwybodaeth 80–90% o'r amser — ond mae cwestiynau uwch-raddol yn arwain at ddysgu dyfnach.

Canfu Walsh a Sattes (2005) bod athrawon fel arfer yn aros llai nag un eiliad ar ôl gofyn cwestiwn. Mae amser aros hirach yn arwain at fwy o ddisgyblion yn llunio atebion.

Inclusive Differentiation Gwahaniaethu Cynhwysol

EAL Learners · Dysgwyr Saesneg fel Ail Iaith (SAY)

🇬🇧 Key Facts about EAL
  • EAL learners will be affected by attitudes towards their culture, language, religion and ethnicity — classroom climate matters.
  • Children can become conversationally fluent in a new language in 2–3 years, but it may take 5 years or longer to match peers in cognitive and academic language.
  • Children benefit hugely from opportunities to continue developing their first language alongside English.
  • EAL learners need to learn English and curriculum content simultaneously. Language functions such as questioning, analysing, and hypothesising must be explicitly taught in each subject.
  • Children learning EAL are as able as any other children. Learning must be no less cognitively challenging — differentiation comes through contextual and linguistic support, not reduced difficulty.
🏴󠁧󠁢󠁷󠁬󠁳󠁿 Cymorth Cyd-destunol ar gyfer Dysgwyr SAY
  • Cyfleoedd i adeiladu ar brofiad blaenorol · Opportunities to build on prior experience
  • Modelu gan yr athro neu ymarferwr · Teacher or practitioner modelling
  • Propiau gweledol a gwrthrychau bywyd go iawn · Visual props and real-life objects
  • Delweddau allweddol: diagramau a llinellau amser · Key visuals: diagrams and timelines
  • Grwpiau cymysg-allu cydweithredol · Opportunities to work in mixed-ability groups
  • Cyfleoedd i ddefnyddio'r iaith gyntaf · Opportunities to use the first language

High cognitive challenge must be maintained through contextual and linguistic support — not reduced difficulty.

SEN Learners · Disgyblion ag Anghenion Dysgu Ychwanegol (ADY)

♿ Key Principles for SEN / ADY

  • Ensure difficulty in one curriculum area does not hold pupils back in others — e.g. present material orally or pictorially for pupils with reading difficulties. / Sicrhewch nad yw anhawster mewn un maes yn atal cynnydd mewn meysydd eraill.
  • For pupils with cognition and learning difficulties, provide more examples, more practice, and more opportunities to generalise concepts across contexts. / Darparwch fwy o enghreifftiau, mwy o ymarfer, a mwy o gyfleoedd i gyffredinoli cysyniadau.
  • Building confidence and self-belief is a crucial priority for pupils with SEN. / Mae adeiladu hyder a hunangred yn flaenoriaeth hollbwysig.
  • Additional adults (LSAs) in the classroom should not always be directed only at lower ability groups — higher ability groups can sometimes be better challenged with the support of an enabling adult. / Ni ddylai oedolion ychwanegol gael eu targedu at grwpiau gallu is yn unig bob amser.

More Able and Talented (MAT) Pupils · Disgyblion Mwy Abl a Thalentog (DAT)

Estyn (2012) — Supporting more able and talented pupils in secondary schools

In the few schools where MAT pupils achieve their full potential, good teaching and learning strategies for MAT pupils impact positively on examination results — and all groups of pupils are appropriately challenged across the curriculum. Effective schools identify strengths and talents through teacher assessment, standardised testing, observation, and parental input, sharing this with all staff. Yn yr ychydig ysgolion lle mae disgyblion DAT yn cyrraedd eu llawn botensial, mae pob grŵp o ddisgyblion hefyd yn cael eu herio'n briodol ar draws y cwricwlwm.

⭐ What MAT pupils benefit from · Yr hyn y mae disgyblion DAT yn elwa ohono

  • Individualised or personalised approaches to learning · Dulliau unigolaidd neu bersonol o ddysgu
  • Access to specialist teachers · Mynediad at athrawon arbenigol
  • Control over how and what they learn · Rheolaeth dros sut a beth maen nhw'n ei ddysgu
  • Discourse that develops techniques for expressing views, posing and interrogating questions · Trafodaeth sy'n datblygu technegau i fynegi barn a chwestiynu
  • Probing questions, peer discussion, and meaningful teacher–pupil interaction in individual lessons · Cwestiynau treiddiol, trafodaeth gyfoedion, a rhyngweithio ystyrlon rhwng athro a disgybl
  • Mentoring to support option and career choices · Mentora i gefnogi dewisiadau opsiynau a gyrfa

As a student teacher, think about which of these you CAN influence within your lessons by planning differentiated challenges for MAT pupils. · Fel myfyriwr athro, meddyliwch am ba rai o'r rhain y gallwch chi ddylanwadu arnynt yn eich gwersi.
