Section 1: What Is the LNF and Why Does It Matter? Adran 1: Beth yw'r FLlRh a Pham Mae'n Bwysig?

🇬🇧 English

The Literacy and Numeracy Framework (LNF) is a statutory cross-curricular skills framework within the Curriculum for Wales. It sets out the literacy and numeracy skills that all learners should develop across all subjects from ages 5–16. As a Humanities teacher, you are a teacher of literacy and numeracy — not just a teacher of History, Geography or RVE.

The LNF is not optional. It is a statutory requirement that sits alongside the Humanities AoLE and must be embedded into your planning, teaching and assessment.

🏴󠁧󠁢󠁷󠁬󠁳󠁿 Cymraeg

Mae'r Fframwaith Llythrennedd a Rhifedd (FLlRh) yn fframwaith sgiliau trawsgwricwlaidd statudol o fewn Cwricwlwm i Gymru. Mae'n nodi'r sgiliau llythrennedd a rhifedd y dylai pob dysgwr eu datblygu ar draws pob pwnc. Fel athro'r Dyniaethau, rydych chi'n athro llythrennedd a rhifedd — nid yn unig athro Hanes, Daearyddiaeth neu GAE.

Nid yw'r FLlRh yn ddewisiol. Mae'n ofyniad statudol sy'n cyd-fynd ag MDP y Dyniaethau.

📌 The Core Principle · Yr Egwyddor Graidd

Every teacher in every subject is responsible for developing learners' literacy and numeracy. The LNF does not ask you to teach separate literacy or numeracy lessons — it asks you to embed these skills naturally into your subject so that Humanities content and cross-curricular skills develop together. / Mae pob athro ym mhob pwnc yn gyfrifol am ddatblygu llythrennedd a rhifedd dysgwyr. Nid yw'r FLlRh yn gofyn i chi addysgu gwersi llythrennedd ar wahan — mae'n gofyn i chi ymgorffori'r sgiliau hyn yn naturiol yn eich pwnc.

Structure of the LNF · Strwythur y FLlRh

📖 LITERACY · LLYTHRENNEDD
Oracy · Llafar — speaking and listening
Reading · Darllen — across all texts and media
Writing · Ysgrifennu — for a range of purposes
🔢 NUMERACY · RHIFEDD
Developing numerical reasoning · Rhesymeg rifiadol — underpins all three
Using number skills · Defnyddio sgiliau rhif

🏠 Where does the LNF fit in the CfW? · Ble mae'r FLlRh yn ffitio yn y CfW?

  • The LNF sits alongside the six Areas of Learning and Experience as a statutory cross-curricular skills framework / Mae'r FLlRh yn eistedd ochr yn ochr a'r chwe Maes Dysgu a Phrofiad fel fframwaith sgiliau trawsgwricwlaidd statudol
  • It connects directly to the CfW's ambition for learners to become ambitious, capable learners — one of the Four Purposes / Mae'n cysylltu'n uniongyrchol ag uchelgais y CfW ar gyfer dysgwyr i ddod yn ddysgwyr uchelgeisiol, galluog
  • Literacy and numeracy skills are not add-ons to Humanities — they are the tools through which pupils do History, Geography and RVE / Nid yw sgiliau llythrennedd a rhifedd yn ategion — maent yn offer y mae disgyblion yn defnyddio i wneud Hanes, Daearyddiaeth a GAE
  • Schools must plan systematically for LNF progression across the curriculum — this is a whole-school responsibility / Rhaid i ysgolion gynllunio'n systematig ar gyfer dilyniant FLlRh

"The LNF is primarily a curriculum planning tool. It is also an assessment tool in that it informs teacher assessment. Schools will need to ensure that whole-school systems are in place to support the consistent and rigorous development of literacy and numeracy skills across all subjects."

— Curriculum for Wales: Literacy and Numeracy Framework (Welsh Government)

Section 2: The Three Literacy Strands Adran 2: Y Tair Ffrwd Llythrennedd

🗣

Oracy · Llafar

Siarad a gwrando

Speaking and listening for different purposes and audiences. Includes discussion, debate, presentation, role play, questioning and responding. In Humanities: class discussions, enquiry dialogue, hot-seating, think-pair-share, debate. / Siarad a gwrando at ddibenion a chynulleidfaoedd gwahanol.

🕮

Reading · Darllen

Deall testunau amrywiol

Reading and understanding a range of continuous and non-continuous texts, print and digital. Includes locating information, inference, evaluation of sources and interpretations. In Humanities: primary sources, maps, statistics, graphs, images, secondary accounts. / Darllen a deall ystod o destunau.

✍

Writing · Ysgrifennu

Ysgrifennu at wahanol ddibenion

Writing for different purposes, audiences and forms: organising ideas and information, and writing accurately. Includes analytical essays, extended responses, structured argument. In Humanities: extended writing, discursive essays, analytical reports, creative responses. / Ysgrifennu at ddibenion, cynulleidfaoedd a ffurfiau gwahanol.

Key aspects within each strand · Agweddau allweddol ym mhob ffrwd

🗣 Oracy — What the LNF expects / Beth mae'r FLlRh yn ei ddisgwyl

Listening and understanding

  • Listening carefully and responding to complex information
  • Identifying key ideas and evaluating what is heard
  • Understanding and responding to different viewpoints

Speaking and presenting

  • Speaking clearly and confidently for different purposes
  • Presenting ideas logically and persuasively
  • Choosing appropriate vocabulary and register
🕮 Reading — What the LNF expects / Beth mae'r FLlRh yn ei ddisgwyl

Locating and using information

  • Retrieving, summarising and synthesising information
  • Reading for different purposes: pleasure, research, critical analysis
  • Interpreting and integrating information from multiple sources

Responding to and analysing

  • Identifying bias, purpose and audience
  • Evaluating reliability and validity of texts
  • Making inferences and identifying implicit meaning
✍ Writing — What the LNF expects / Beth mae'r FLlRh yn ei ddisgwyl

Organising ideas and information

  • Planning and structuring writing for different purposes
  • Selecting and deploying relevant information
  • Using appropriate forms, genres and structures

Writing accurately

  • Using accurate grammar, spelling and punctuation
  • Developing vocabulary appropriate to subject context
  • Using connectives and discourse markers to aid cohesion

Section 3: The Four Numeracy Strands Adran 3: Y Pedair Ffrwd Rhifedd

🔎 An important distinction · Gwahaniaeth pwysig

Numeracy in the LNF is not the same as mathematics. It is the ability to apply numerical skills and reasoning to real-world contexts — including the contexts of History, Geography and RVE. You are not expected to teach mathematics; you are expected to use numerical information purposefully and to develop learners' confidence and accuracy when doing so in your subject. / Nid yw rhifedd yn yr FLlRh yr un fath a mathemateg. Mae'n allu i gymhwyso sgiliau a rhesymeg rifiadol i gyd-destunau byd go iawn.

🧠

Developing Numerical Reasoning

Datblygu Rhesymeg Rifiadol

Underpins all three other strands. Identifying mathematical strategies, representing and communicating findings, reviewing and improving. This is about thinking numerically — asking good questions about data, spotting patterns, challenging conclusions. / Yn sylfaen i'r tair ffrwd arall. Mae hwn am feddwl yn rifiadol.

🔢

Using Number Skills

Defnyddio Sgiliau Rhif

Using and applying numbers in context: percentages, fractions, ratios, estimation. In Humanities: population statistics, historical date calculations, percentage change (population growth, mortality rates, land use), electoral data. / Defnyddio a chymhwyso rhifau mewn cyd-destun: canrannau, ffracsiynau, cymharebau.

📏

Using Measuring Skills

Defnyddio Sgiliau Mesur

Measuring, estimating and recording in appropriate units. In Geography this is particularly prominent: map scales, distances, area, temperature, rainfall, river discharge. In History: dimensions of historical sites, timelines to scale. / Mesur, amcangyfrif a chofnodi. Yn amlwg iawn mewn Daearyddiaeth: graddfeydd map, pellter, arwynebedd.

📊

Using Data Skills

Defnyddio Sgiliau Data

Collecting, representing, analysing and interpreting data. In Humanities: population graphs, climate data, historical census data, survey results, choropleth maps, pie charts of religious affiliation. Reading and critiquing data representations. / Casglu, cynrychioli, dadansoddi a dehongli data.

Numeracy in the three Humanities subjects · Rhifedd yn y tair disgyblaeth

🕼 History · Hanes
  • Timeline construction and calculation
  • Population data: census, mortality, migration rates
  • Percentage change over time
  • Interpreting graphs showing economic change
  • Electoral data and voting patterns
  • Scale and chronology
🌎 Geography · Daearyddiaeth
  • Map scales and grid references
  • Climate graphs and rainfall data
  • Population pyramids
  • River discharge and flooding statistics
  • Choropleth and proportional symbol maps
  • Fieldwork data collection and analysis
☮ RVE · GAE
  • Statistics on religious affiliation in Wales and globally
  • Survey data on religious belief
  • Interpreting census data on faith communities
  • Timelines of religious events and movements
  • Comparing demographic trends across faith groups
  • Ethical data: poverty statistics, global inequalities

Section 4: The LNF in History, Geography and RVE Adran 4: Y FLlRh mewn Hanes, Daearyddiaeth a GAE

Each subject in the Humanities AoLE has its own particular relationship with the LNF strands. The examples below show how LNF skills are not external impositions — they are how the disciplines actually work. / Mae gan bob pwnc yn MDP y Dyniaethau ei berthynas arbennig ei hun gyda ffrydiau'r FLlRh.

🕼
History · Hanes
Ymholiad hanesyddol a sgiliau trawsgwricwlaidd
Oracy
Historical debate: "Was the Treaty of Versailles the main cause of World War Two?" Pupils present a structured spoken argument, listen critically to opposing views and use evidence to rebut. Develops speaking for a purpose, use of historical vocabulary and listening to evaluate. Dadl hanesyddol: disgyblion yn cyflwyno dadl lafar strwythuredig, yn gwrando'n feirniadol ac yn defnyddio tystiolaeth i wrthbrofi.
Reading
Source analysis using a 1917 Welsh coalfield photograph. Pupils locate information, make inferences about working conditions, evaluate the provenance and purpose of the source. Directly addresses locating information, inference and evaluating reliability. Dadansoddi ffynhonnell: disgyblion yn lleoli gwybodaeth, yn gwneud casgliadau am amodau gwaith, yn gwerthuso tarddiad y ffynhonnell.
Writing
Structured analytical essay: "How far was the Rebecca Riots a response to poverty rather than toll gates?" Pupils plan, draft and write a substantiated argument using Counsell's sorting and writing frame strategies. Traethawd dadansoddol strwythuredig gyda fframwaith ysgrifennu: disgyblion yn cynllunio, drafftio ac ysgrifennu dadl sylweddol.
Data
Interpreting a graph showing the decline of Welsh-speaking population 1901–2021. Pupils read percentage data, identify trends and connect to historical causes (industrialisation, migration, language policy). Develops data skills within a historical context. Dehongli graff yn dangos dirywiad poblogaeth Cymraeg ei hiaith 1901-2021: darllen data canrannol, nodi tueddiadau a chysylltu ag achosion hanesyddol.
Reasoning
Evaluating a historical claim: "Did the number of deaths at Senghennydd prove that coal mine safety was inadequate?" Pupils assess what the statistic shows and what it does not show, and what additional evidence would be needed. Gwerthuso honiad hanesyddol: disgyblion yn asesu beth mae'r ystadegyn yn ei brofi a beth nad yw'n ei brofi.
🌎
Geography · Daearyddiaeth
Ymholiad daearyddol a sgiliau data ac iaith
Oracy
Geographical enquiry discussion: after studying flood risk maps of Cardiff, pupils use Think-Pair-Share to discuss: "Is flooding in the South Wales Valleys primarily a physical or human problem?" Developing use of geographical vocabulary, structured argument and listening to evaluate evidence. Trafodaeth ymholiad daearyddol: disgyblion yn defnyddio Meddwl-Paru-Rhannu i drafod perygl llifogydd.
Reading
Reading and interpreting an OS map of a Welsh coastal area to identify land use, relief and human features. Pupils locate, infer and evaluate geographical information from a non-continuous text. Directly addresses reading across different formats and media. Darllen a dehongli map OS: lleoli, casglu a gwerthuso gwybodaeth ddaearyddol o destun amharhaus.
Writing
Report writing: "Should the Welsh Government invest in managed coastal retreat at [local site]?" Pupils write a structured geographical report presenting evidence from multiple sources, with a reasoned recommendation. Addresses organising ideas and writing for purpose and audience. Ysgrifennu adroddiad: "A ddylai Llywodraeth Cymru fuddsoddi mewn enciliad arfordirol rheoledig?" Disgyblion yn cyflwyno tystiolaeth ac argymhelliad rhesymegol.
Measure
Using a 1:25,000 OS map to calculate the distance between two points using the scale bar, and estimating journey time. Connects measuring skills to real-world geographical decision-making. Defnyddio map OS 1:25,000 i gyfrifo pellter gan ddefnyddio'r bar graddfa, ac amcangyfrif amser teithio.
Data
Constructing and interpreting a climate graph for Cardiff and a contrasting location. Pupils collect data, plot and read a dual-axis graph, and write a comparison that draws on the data. Develops all four aspects of data skills: collect, represent, analyse, interpret. Adeiladu a dehongli graff hinsawdd ar gyfer Caerdydd a lleoliad cyferbyniol.
☮
Religion, Values and Ethics (RVE) · Crefydd, Gwerthoedd a Moeseg (GAE)
Ymholiad moesegol a sgiliau cyfathrebu sensitif
Oracy
Philosophical enquiry discussion on an ethical dilemma: "Is it ever right to break the law if you believe it is unjust?" Pupils practise structured listening, building on others' contributions, using connective language and religious/ethical vocabulary with precision. Develops extended speaking and critical listening. Trafodaeth ymholiad athronyddol: disgyblion yn ymarfer gwrando strwythuredig, adeiladu ar gyfraniadau eraill, defnyddio geirfa grefyddol/foesegol yn fanwl gywir.
Reading
Reading a Buddhist text alongside a contemporary commentary on environmental ethics, and a news article on climate action. Pupils integrate information across three different text types and identify how different sources are shaped by their purpose and audience. Darllen testun Bwdhaidd a sylwebaeth gyfoes ar foeseg amgylcheddol: integreiddio gwybodaeth ar draws tri math gwahanol o destun.
Writing
Writing a balanced discursive essay: "Do religious teachings on the sanctity of life always provide clear guidance on end-of-life decisions?" Pupils must organise competing perspectives, acknowledge complexity and reach a substantiated conclusion. Addresses all aspects of writing: organising ideas, appropriate genre, accuracy. Ysgrifennu traethawd traethodol cytbwys: disgyblion yn trefnu safbwyntiau cystadleuol, cydnabod cymhlethdod a chyrraedd casgliad sylweddol.
Data
Analysing Census 2021 data on religious affiliation in Wales and England: reading pie charts and bar graphs showing the rise of 'no religion', decline of Christianity and distribution of minority faiths. Pupils connect data to contextual understanding of secularisation. Dadansoddi data Cyfrifiad 2021 ar ymrwymiad crefyddol yng Nghymru a Lloegr: darllen siartiau cylch a graffiau bar yn dangos codiad 'dim crefydd'.
Reasoning
Evaluating global poverty statistics in the context of religious teachings on stewardship: "Does the fact that 700 million people live in extreme poverty prove that religious teachings on care for others have failed?" Pupils assess what the statistic shows and its relationship to religious ethical claims. Gwerthuso ystadegau tlodi byd-eang yng nghyd-destun dysgeidiaeth grefyddol am stiwardiaeth.

Section 5: Planning for LNF & Avoiding Common Pitfalls Adran 5: Cynllunio ar gyfer y FLlRh ac Osgoi Peryglon Cyffredin

🇬🇧 English

LNF skills should emerge naturally from strong subject teaching — not be bolted on artificially. The most effective practice is when a teacher plans to develop a specific LNF skill because it is the skill that subject content demands, not because a box needs to be ticked. The question to ask is always: "What literacy or numeracy skill does engaging with this historical/geographical/ethical material actually require?"

🏴󠁧󠁢󠁷󠁬󠁳󠁿 Cymraeg

Dylai sgiliau FLlRh godi'n naturiol o addysgu pwnc cryf — nid eu hategion yn artiffisial. Y ymarfer mwyaf effeithiol yw pan fydd athro'n cynllunio i ddatblygu sgil FLlRh penodol oherwydd mai dyna'r sgil y mae cynnwys y pwnc yn ei ofyn. Y cwestiwn i'w ofyn bob amser yw: "Pa sgil llythrennedd neu rifedd y mae ymgysylltu gyda'r deunydd hwn yn ei ofyn?"

Common Pitfalls · Peryglon Cyffredin

❌ Avoid · Osgowch
  • Tokenism — adding a graph or word bank to a lesson and calling it LNF. If pupils are not actually developing the skill, there is no LNF benefit. / Toceniaeth — ychwanegu graff neu fanc geiriau at wers a'i galw'n FLlRh.
  • Literacy for its own sake — extended writing that has no relationship to the historical/geographical/ethical enquiry. If the writing does not develop subject understanding, it is not serving the LNF or the subject. / Llythrennedd er ei mwyn ei hun.
  • Treating oracy as chat — unstructured discussion is not oracy in the LNF sense. Oracy requires pupils to develop speaking for a purpose, listening critically and responding to evidence. / Trin llafar fel sgwrs anstrwythuredig.
  • Ignoring numeracy — many Humanities teachers underestimate how much numeracy their subject demands. Learners who cannot read a graph or interpret a percentage will be limited in their engagement with real historical and geographical evidence. / Anwybyddu rhifedd.
  • One-off activities — a single lesson on graph reading does not develop data skills. LNF requires sustained, progressive development across the year. / Gweithgareddau untro — nid yw un wers ar ddarllen graff yn datblygu sgiliau data.
✓ Instead, do this · Yn hytrach, gwnewch hyn
  • Plan backwards — identify the LNF skill that your enquiry question genuinely demands, then build activities that develop it through subject content. / Cynllunio'n ôl — nodi'r sgil FLlRh y mae eich cwestiwn ymholi'n ei ofyn go iawn.
  • Make the skill explicit — tell learners which literacy or numeracy skill they are developing and why it matters in this subject. Metacognitive awareness improves performance. / Gwneud y sgil yn benodol — dweud wrth ddysgwyr pa sgil y maent yn ei datblygu.
  • Use subject content to teach the skill — a historical source is the ideal vehicle for developing inference and evaluation skills, not just source content. / Defnyddio cynnwys y pwnc i addysgu'r sgil.
  • Plan for progression — note which LNF skills you are developing in each scheme of work, and ensure learners encounter them repeatedly and with increasing complexity. / Cynllunio ar gyfer dilyniant — nodi pa sgiliau FLlRh rydych yn eu datblygu ym mhob cynllun gwaith.
  • Collaborate with your department — LNF planning should be a departmental responsibility, not a lone teacher's task. A skills map across KS3 and KS4 ensures coherence. / Cydweithio gyda'ch adran — dylai cynllunio'r FLlRh fod yn gyfrifoldeb adrannol.

Annotating a lesson plan for LNF · Anodi cynllun gwers ar gyfer y FLlRh

📌 A simple annotation system · System anodi syml

When planning a lesson, annotate each activity with the LNF strand it addresses. Use shorthand: O (Oracy), R (Reading), W (Writing), NR (Numerical Reasoning), N (Number), M (Measuring), D (Data). If you finish planning a lesson and there are no annotations at all, that is a signal to think about whether you are missing LNF opportunities — not to add them artificially, but to notice what the subject material naturally offers. / Wrth gynllunio gwers, anodwch bob gweithgaredd gyda'r ffrwd FLlRh y mae'n ei fynd i'r afael.
