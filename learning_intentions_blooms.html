Rhan A · Part A: Key Concepts for Reference Cysyniadau Allweddol ar gyfer Cyfeirio

🇬🇧 English

A learning intention describes what pupils will KNOW, UNDERSTAND or be able to DO as a result of the lesson. It must contain a Bloom's verb. Success criteria are the observable actions that show a learner has achieved it.

The task is the vehicle. The learning intention is the destination. The success criteria are the road signs.

🏴󠁧󠁢󠁷󠁬󠁳󠁿 Cymraeg

Mae bwriad dysgu yn disgrifio beth fydd disgyblion yn ei WYBOD, DDEALL neu allu ei WNEUD o ganlyniad i'r wers. Rhaid iddo gynnwys berf Bloom. Mae meini prawf llwyddiant yn weithredoedd gweladwy sy'n dangos bod dysgwr wedi ei gyflawni.

Y dasg yw'r cerbyd. Y bwriad dysgu yw'r cyrchfan. Y meini prawf llwyddiant yw'r arwyddion ffordd.

⚠️ The Bloom's Rule · Rheol Bloom

"Pupils will be able to [BLOOM'S VERB] + [CONTENT] + [CONTEXT / DEPTH]"

If you cannot point to a Bloom's verb in your learning intention, it is not a learning intention yet. · Os na allwch chi nodi berf Bloom yn eich bwriad dysgu, nid yw'n fwriad dysgu eto.

The Four Common Mistakes · Y Pedwar Camgymeriad Cyffredin

1. Describing the task, not the learning

❌ Pupils will draw a diagram of the water cycle
✅ Pupils will EXPLAIN how water moves through the hydrological cycle

2. Using vague non-Bloom's verbs

❌ Pupils will know about coastal erosion
✅ Pupils will ANALYSE how hydraulic action shapes a cliff face

3. Setting the ceiling too low

❌ Pupils will LIST features of a mosque (Year 9)
✅ Pupils will EVALUATE how mosque design expresses Islamic belief

4. Writing too many intentions

❌ Three or four LIs for one lesson
✅ One clear Bloom's LI per lesson — the SC break it down

📋 Your task · Eich tasg

Each statement below is NOT a genuine Bloom's-driven learning intention. Rewrite each one using the rule: 'Pupils will be able to [BLOOM'S VERB] + [CONTENT] + [CONTEXT/DEPTH]'

📜 History · Hanes

❌ R1 · History · Hanes — As Written · Fel yr Ysgrifennwyd

The Industrial Revolution in Wales

This is a topic heading — no Bloom's verb and no indication of what learners will DO. Choose a Bloom's level and rewrite it. · Pennawd pwnc yw hwn — dim berf Bloom ac dim arwydd o beth fydd dysgwyr yn ei WNEUD.
❌ R2 · History · Hanes — As Written · Fel yr Ysgrifennwyd

Pupils will know about the suffragette movement and why women wanted the vote.

'Know about' is not a Bloom's verb. Reframe this at Bloom's level 4 or 5 — what should learners DO with this knowledge? · Nid yw 'gwybod am' yn berf Bloom. Ailframiwch hwn ar lefel 4 neu 5.
❌ R3 · History · Hanes — As Written · Fel yr Ysgrifennwyd

Pupils will complete a mind map of the causes of the First World War.

This describes the task (a mind map), not the learning. What is the learning the mind map is MEANT to produce? Write the LI for that learning. · Mae hwn yn disgrifio'r dasg, nid y dysgu. Beth yw'r dysgu yr oedd y map meddwl i'w gynhyrchu?

🌏 Geography · Daearyddiaeth

❌ R4 · Geography · Daearyddiaeth — As Written · Fel yr Ysgrifennwyd

Climate change — causes and effects

Topic heading with no verb. For Year 9, what Bloom's level (4, 5 or 6) is most appropriate? Rewrite as a full LI. · Pennawd pwnc heb ferf. Ar gyfer Blwyddyn 9, pa lefel Bloom sy'n fwyaf priodol?
❌ R5 · Geography · Daearyddiaeth — As Written · Fel yr Ysgrifennwyd

Pupils will learn about urbanisation in Wales and what it means for communities.

'Learn about' is not a Bloom's verb. Pick a more precise verb from level 3–5 and rewrite so the LI is specific and assessable. · Nid yw 'dysgu am' yn berf Bloom. Dewiswch ferf mwy manwl o lefel 3–5.
❌ R6 · Geography · Daearyddiaeth — As Written · Fel yr Ysgrifennwyd

Pupils will look at maps of the Welsh coastline and identify areas at risk from flooding.

Close, but describes a task. Push it to Bloom's level 5 — what should learners DO with the flood risk data? · Yn agos, ond yn disgrifio tasg. Gwthiwch i lefel 5 Bloom — beth ddylai dysgwyr ei WNEUD â'r data risg llifogydd?

🕊️ RVE · Crefydd, Gwerthoedd ac Moeseg

❌ R7 · RVE · Crefydd, Gwerthoedd ac Moeseg — As Written · Fel yr Ysgrifennwyd

Life after death — different religious views

Topic heading. RVE at Year 9 should demand Bloom's level 5 or 6. Rewrite so learners must evaluate or justify, not just describe. · Pennawd pwnc. Dylai GAE ym Mlwyddyn 9 ofyn am lefel 5 neu 6 Bloom.
❌ R8 · RVE · Crefydd, Gwerthoedd ac Moeseg — As Written · Fel yr Ysgrifennwyd

Pupils will understand how Christian beliefs affect the way people live.

'Understand' is Bloom's level 2 but probably aimed too low for this content. What would level 4 or 5 look like? Rewrite it. · Mae 'deall' ar lefel 2 Bloom ond mae'n rhy isel ar gyfer y cynnwys hwn. Sut olwg fyddai ar lefel 4 neu 5?
❌ R9 · RVE · Crefydd, Gwerthoedd ac Moeseg — As Written · Fel yr Ysgrifennwyd

Pupils will write a leaflet explaining what Sikhs believe about equality.

The task (leaflet) is not the learning. What is the learning BEHIND this task? Write the LI the leaflet is meant to demonstrate. · Nid y dasg (taflen) yw'r dysgu. Beth yw'r dysgu y TU ÔL i'r dasg hon?
