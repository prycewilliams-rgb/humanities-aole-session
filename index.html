Welcome to the Session

Croeso i'r Sesiwn
English

Welcome to your first exploration of the Humanities Area of Learning and Experience (AoLE) — one of the six areas that form the Curriculum for Wales framework. This session will help you understand what the Humanities AoLE is, why it matters, and what it means for your teaching.

By the end of today, you will be able to describe the vision and structure of the area, identify its five Statements of What Matters, and begin to see how they connect to the Four Purposes of the curriculum.

The Big Picture

Y Darlun Mawr — Cwricwlwm i Gymru
English

The Curriculum for Wales, implemented from 2022 onwards, replaces a subject-based approach with six interconnected Areas of Learning and Experience (AoLEs). At the heart of everything are the Four Purposes, which describe the aspirations for every learner in Wales.

Cymraeg

Mae Cwricwlwm i Gymru, a weithredwyd o 2022 ymlaen, yn disodli dull pwnc-seiliedig gyda chwe Maes Dysgu a Phrofiad (MDPh) rhyng-gysylltiedig. Wrth wraidd popeth mae'r Pedwar Diben, sy'n disgrifio'r dyheadau ar gyfer pob dysgwr yng Nghymru.

The Humanities AoLE — Vision / Gweledigaeth

English — from the Hwb guidance

"The Humanities Area of Learning and Experience seeks to awaken a sense of wonder, fire the imagination and inspire learners to grow in knowledge, understanding and wisdom. This Area encourages learners to engage with the most important issues facing humanity, including sustainability and social change, and helps develop the skills necessary to interpret and articulate the past and the present."

Cymraeg — o arweiniad Hwb

"Mae Maes Dysgu a Phrofiad y Dyniaethau yn ceisio deffro ymdeimlad o ryfeddod, tanio'r dychymyg ac ysbrydoli dysgwyr i dyfu mewn gwybodaeth, dealltwriaeth a doethineb. Mae'r Maes hwn yn annog dysgwyr i ymgysylltu â'r materion pwysicaf sy'n wynebu dynoliaeth, gan gynnwys cynaliadwyedd a newid cymdeithasol."

The Five Statements of What Matters

Y Pum Datganiad o'r Hyn sy'n Bwysig
English

The five Statements of What Matters are the statutory big ideas at the heart of Humanities. They are highly interconnected and must not be treated in isolation. Together they move from building skills → exploring perspectives → understanding the world → taking action.

Cymraeg

Mae'r pum Datganiad o'r Hyn sy'n Bwysig yn syniadau mawr statudol wrth wraidd y Dyniaethau. Maent yn rhyng-gysylltiedig iawn ac ni ddylid eu trin ar wahân. Gyda'i gilydd, maent yn symud o adeiladu sgiliau → archwilio safbwyntiau → deall y byd → cymryd camau.

1

Enquiry & Discovery / Ymholi a Darganfod

The enquiry and discovery of the nature of our world, our past and our present enable us to make sense of and improve our world.

Mae ymholi a darganfod natur ein byd, ein gorffennol a'n presennol yn ein galluogi i wneud synnwyr o'r byd a'i wella.
2

Perspectives & Interpretation / Safbwyntiau a Dehongliad

The variety of ways in which we see, interpret and represent our world and human experience influences all our values and choices.

Mae amrywiaeth y ffyrdd y gwelwn, dehonglwn ac yn cynrychioli ein byd a phrofiad dynol yn dylanwadu ar ein holl werthoedd a dewisiadau.
3

The Natural World / Y Byd Naturiol

Our natural world is diverse and dynamic, influenced by physical processes and human actions.

Mae ein byd naturiol yn amrywiol a deinamig, wedi'i ddylanwadu gan brosesau ffisegol a chamau dynol.
4

Human Societies / Cymdeithasau Dynol

Human societies are complex and diverse, and shaped by human actions and beliefs.

Mae cymdeithasau dynol yn gymhleth ac yn amrywiol, wedi'u llunio gan weithredoedd a chredoau dynol.
5

Ethical & Sustainable Action / Camau Moesegol a Chynaliadwy

Informed, self-aware citizens engage with the challenges and opportunities that face humanity and are able to take considered, ethical and sustainable action.

Mae dinasyddion gwybodus, ymwybodol ohonynt eu hunain yn ymgysylltu â'r heriau a'r cyfleoedd sy'n wynebu dynoliaeth ac yn gallu cymryd camau ystyriol, moesegol a chynaliadwy.

Key Concept: Cynefin

Cynefin

A uniquely Welsh concept describing the place where we feel we belong — where the people and landscape around us are familiar, and the sights and sounds are reassuringly recognisable. It encompasses habitat, environment, and a profound sense of home. In the Humanities AoLE, cultivating a learner's sense of cynefin grounds their understanding of Wales, its communities, history and culture, providing a foundation from which they can confidently engage with the wider world.

Y lle rydym yn perthyn iddo — y gymuned leol, hanes a diwylliant sy'n llunio pwy ydym ni.

Structure of the Statements / Strwythur y Datganiadau

📐 Remember: Statements 1–2 are skills-focused. Statements 3–4 are knowledge-focused. Statement 5 is a call to action. This unique structure reflects the AoLE's ambition: not just to inform, but to equip learners to act as responsible citizens.

Task 3 / Tasg 3 Scenario Analysis / Dadansoddi Senarios ⏱ 20 mins (pair work / gwaith pâr)

Read each classroom scenario below. Identify which Statement(s) of What Matters and which disciplines are present. Discuss with a partner then write your analysis. / Darllenwch bob senario. Nodwch pa Datganiad(au) o'r Hyn sy'n Bwysig a pha ddisgyblaethau sydd yn bresennol.

🏭

Scenario A — Y Chwyldro Diwydiannol / The Industrial Revolution

Year 9 learners investigate how coal mining in South Wales shaped communities in their local area. They compare oral testimonies from mining families with economic data about the decline of the industry and debate what responsibility today's government has to former mining communities.

🌊

Scenario B — Newid yn yr Hinsawdd / Climate Change

Year 7 learners explore how rising sea levels may affect coastal communities in Wales. They examine physical geography processes, analyse the perspectives of local residents, environmental scientists and business owners, then design a community response plan.

🕌

Scenario C — Cymunedau Crefyddol / Religious Communities

Year 8 learners examine how different religious and non-religious worldviews interpret justice and human rights. They hear from members of local faith communities, study the Universal Declaration of Human Rights, and write a reflective piece on how values shape laws.

For each scenario, answer: (1) Which Statements of What Matters are addressed? (2) Which disciplines are involved? (3) How does it connect to the Four Purposes? / Ar gyfer pob senario, atebwch: (1) Pa Ddatganiadau? (2) Pa ddisgyblaethau? (3) Sut mae'n cysylltu â'r Pedwar Diben?
Task 4 / Tasg 4 Mini Curriculum Map / Map Cwricwlwm Mini ⏱ 20 mins

Choose a topic relevant to your subject specialism (e.g. the Slave Trade, River Flooding, Migration, World War One, Identity & Belonging). Map out how a unit of work on that topic could address the Humanities AoLE. / Dewiswch destun sy'n berthnasol i'ch arbenigedd pwnc. Mapio sut y gallai uned waith fynd i'r afael â'r Maes Dyniaethau.

Think about: Which Statements of What Matters does your topic address? Which cross-curricular skills (literacy, numeracy, digital) could be developed? How does it connect to the learners' cynefin or the story of Wales? / Sut mae'n cysylltu â chynefin y dysgwyr neu stori Cymru?

Reflection & Consolidation

Adlewyrchu a Chadarnhau
English

Take some time to consolidate what you have learned today. Effective teachers in Wales must understand not just what they teach, but why the curriculum is designed as it is, and how their subject contributes to the broader purposes of education in Wales.

Cymraeg

Cymerwch ychydig o amser i gadarnhau'r hyn rydych chi wedi'i ddysgu heddiw. Rhaid i athrawon effeithiol yng Nghymru ddeall nid yn unig beth maen nhw'n ei addysgu, ond pam mae'r cwricwlwm wedi'i gynllunio fel y mae.

Key Learning Checklist / Rhestr Wirio Dysgu Allweddol

  • I can explain what the Humanities AoLE is and name its core disciplines. / Gallaf egluro beth yw MDP y Dyniaethau a henwi ei ddisgyblaethau craidd.
  • I can describe the vision of the Humanities AoLE in my own words. / Gallaf ddisgrifio gweledigaeth MDP y Dyniaethau yn fy ngeiriau fy hun.
  • I can name and broadly describe the Five Statements of What Matters. / Gallaf enwi a disgrifio'r Pum Datganiad o'r Hyn sy'n Bwysig yn fras.
  • I understand the significance of cynefin within the Humanities AoLE. / Rwy'n deall arwyddocâd cynefin yn MDP y Dyniaethau.
  • I can explain how the Four Purposes relate to Humanities. / Gallaf egluro sut mae'r Pedwar Diben yn ymwneud â'r Dyniaethau.
  • I can begin to identify how my subject specialism contributes to the AoLE. / Gallaf ddechrau nodi sut mae fy arbenigedd pwnc yn cyfrannu at y MDP.

Final Reflection Questions / Cwestiynau Adlewyrchu Terfynol

🤔 Reflection 1 / Adlewyrchiad 1

What surprised you most about the structure or content of the Humanities AoLE? How does it differ from what you expected based on your own school experience? / Beth oedd yn eich synnu fwyaf am strwythur neu gynnwys MDP y Dyniaethau?

🏫 Reflection 2 / Adlewyrchiad 2

Think about your placement school. How do you think they are currently approaching the Humanities AoLE? What questions do you want to ask your mentor about curriculum design? / Sut ydych chi'n meddwl bod eich ysgol leoliad yn mynd i'r afael â MDP y Dyniaethau ar hyn o bryd?

🌿 Reflection 3 / Adlewyrchiad 3 — Cynefin

What is your cynefin? Describe the place, community or landscape that gives you a sense of belonging. How might this concept help you build connections with the learners you will teach? / Beth yw eich cynefin chi? Sut allai'r cysyniad hwn eich helpu i adeiladu cysylltiadau â dysgwyr?

Next Steps / Camau Nesaf
